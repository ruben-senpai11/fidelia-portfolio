The 8 elements of a converting Landing Page : 
1. Engaging hero shot
2. use of a video to describe what is being sold
3. Detailed lists of benefits that typically could be explained by someone clicking ...see more
4. Testimonials with the image of the person and the product
5. Call to action that is more related to the product versus using a generic one
6. high quality images with text overlaid on top that explained the benefits of the product
7. Subscribe and save options
8. Showcasing all the payment options by displaying logos
